# Aida AI Website Builder & AI Assistant Knowledge Base

## General Procedures & Policies

### 1. What is Aida AI Assistant & Website Builder
- **Overview:** Aida is an integrated AI assistant built into one.com's products, inspired by the visionary mathematician Ada Lovelace. It is designed to automate time-consuming tasks like website building, domain discovery, content creation, and email writing.
- **Aida AI Assistant Features (Included at No Extra Cost):**
  - **Domain Discovery:** Generates and explores unique, relevant domain names based on a business description.
  - **Professional Email Assistant:** Instantly generates polished, professional email drafts in **11 languages** for freelancers and small businesses.
  - **AI-Powered SEO:** Suggests keywords, optimizes on-page structure, and writes SEO-friendly content to boost search engine rankings.
  - **Multi-Platform Integration:** Works across different platforms, including the dedicated AI Website Builder and as an AI assistant for WordPress.
- **Aida AI Website Builder:** one.com's next-generation AI-powered website builder. Users describe what they want in plain language via a conversational AI chat, and Aida generates the layout, copy, structure, and includes secure European hosting, Online Shop, and Bookings.
- **Key Difference from Classic Website Builder:**
  - Classic Website Builder = drag-and-drop, template-based, manual component placement.
  - Aida AI Website Builder = conversational AI-first, with a visual editor for fine-tuning.
- **Login URL:** `https://aida.one.com`

### 2. Three Ways to Get Started
- **Start with an Example:** Choose a ready-made starting point for inspiration. Best for users unsure where to begin.
- **Describe Your Idea:** Write a freeform prompt (e.g., "Create a website for my yoga studio"). Best for new businesses wanting a personalised site.
- **Modernise Your Website:** Paste the URL of an existing website and Aida rebuilds it with a modern design, preserving images/videos/content. Best for outdated sites or quick redesigns.

### 3. Plans and Pricing
- **No Extra Cost for General Assistant Features:** General Aida AI assistant features (like domain discovery or the email assistant) are built-in within one.com's core products at **no added cost**.
- **Three Website Builder Plans:** Portfolio, Booking, Commerce.
- **Portfolio:** 1 email inbox (3 GB), 25 GB storage, unlimited pages, 1 website, 1 free domain (1st year). No bookings or commerce.
- **Booking:** 5 email inboxes (3 GB each), 50 GB storage, 25 appointment bookings/month, AI bookings setup, calendar integration (Google/Outlook).
- **Commerce:** 5 email inboxes (3 GB each), 100 GB storage, 50 appointment bookings/month, full AI-powered shop (product management, shipping, Shore Pay, order management, 0% transaction fees).
- **Important:** Aida AI Website Builder plans are separate products from classic Website Builder plans. Cannot be purchased as an add-on.
- **Free Trial:** A free trial is available (no credit card required) to build and test the AI website builder before committing to a paid plan.
- **AI Usage Limits:** Fair use limit applies (not strictly defined). Users get notifications at ~80% usage and at the limit. Resets monthly. No top-up option available.

### 4. Building and Editing Your Site
- **Conversational AI Chat:** Primary interface. Users ask Aida to change sections, layouts, rewrite text, update colours/themes, add pages/features. No coding or prompt-writing expertise needed.
- **Visual Editor:** For fine-tuning after AI generation. Available via "Manual edit" button or pencil icon in toolbar.
  - **Edit Text:** Double-click text → change directly, ask Aida for changes, or adjust styling (size, alignment, colours).
  - **Edit Images:** Click image → "Ask for changes" (AI generates replacement) or "Upload" (own image).
  - **Add Sections:** Via right-side toolbar or by asking Aida. Categories include Essential, Business, Online Shop, Engagement, Media, Contact, Trust.
- **Chat-Only Features (at launch):** Navigation changes, widget integration, video background, embed code, Instagram feed/gallery, basic SEO settings (per-page metadata, sitemap.xml, robots.txt).
- **Responsive Preview:** Click device icon in top bar to toggle Desktop, Tablet, or Mobile view.

### 5. Publishing and Domain Setup
- **Publishing Flow:** Click "Publish" → purchase plan + domain → pick domain (new or transfer) → go live.
- **Domain Options:**
  - New domain: Free for first year on certain TLDs.
  - Transfer from another provider: Supported, but takes time; manual publish required after transfer completes.
  - **Limitation:** Existing domains already registered with one.com CANNOT currently be connected to an Aida AI Website Builder site. Must purchase a new domain. (Feature in development.)
- **Auto-created Email:** When a domain is purchased, `info@yourdomain.com` is automatically created. All contact form and shop emails go here.
- **Propagation Time:** Up to 20 minutes after domain purchase before the site becomes accessible.
- **"Coming Soon" Overlay:** Users can ask Aida to add a temporary overlay to hide the site while work continues. Publish with overlay, then ask Aida to remove it when ready.

### 6. Key Limitations
- **No Existing Domain Connection (one.com domains):** Cannot connect a domain already registered with one.com to an Aida AI site. Must buy a new domain.
- **No File Export:** Cannot export or save website files, extract colour hex codes, or retrieve uploaded photos from the Aida platform.
- **No Site Migration:** Cannot move an existing classic Website Builder site to Aida AI Website Builder. Workaround: use "Modernise your website" to recreate the site in Aida.
- **Not for Large Businesses:** Not suited for businesses with existing advanced customisation, complex workflows, or multiple websites.
- **Switching Plans:** Can upgrade/downgrade from the one.com Control Panel → "Change plan."

### 7. AI Guidance and SEO
- **Next-Best Actions:** Aida can analyse the current site and suggest improvements. Users discuss options with Aida and ask it to execute changes.
- **Basic SEO Settings:** Per-page metadata (title, description, OG), sitemap.xml, robots.txt — all managed via AI chat only.
- **AI Content Generation:** Blog content, SEO-friendly copy, and website text can be generated through conversation.

### 8. Bookings and Commerce Features
- **Bookings (Booking & Commerce plans):**
  - AI-powered bookings setup and management via chat.
  - Service management, appointment management, repeat appointments.
  - Calendar views, customer creation at booking, confirmation/cancellation emails.
  - Calendar integration with Google and Outlook.
  - Booking limits: 25/month (Booking plan), 50/month (Commerce plan).
- **Commerce (Commerce plan only):**
  - AI shop setup, product management, shop settings.
  - Shipping (local & custom), Shore Pay for payments, order management.
  - 0% transaction fees.

### 9. Security and Hosting
- **All Plans Include:** SSL certificates, DDoS protection, WAF, DNSSEC, 14-day backup and restore, versioning (full design/code history), IPv6, 24/7 server monitoring.
- **Hosting Specs:** 2x CPU priority, unlimited data traffic, SSH/SFTP access, mod_rewrite, PHP 8+.
- **Storage:** 25 GB (Portfolio), 50 GB (Booking), 100 GB (Commerce).
- **Databases:** 1 MariaDB (Portfolio), 5 (Booking), 10 (Commerce).
