# Website Builder (Classic) Knowledge Base

## General Procedures & Policies

### 1. Getting Started with Website Builder — Overview
- **Product:** one.com's classic drag-and-drop, template-based Website Builder (distinct from the Aida AI Website Builder).
- **Access:** Log in to the one.com Control Panel → click the **Website Builder** tab in the left menu → select **Website Builder**.
- **Trial Users:** Trial version is accessed via a separate login URL: `https://try-websitebuilder.one.com/#login`.
- **Key Workflow (9 Steps):**
  1. Find Website Builder in the Control Panel.
  2. Add basic business/project information (AI-driven onboarding).
  3. Choose a colour theme and template.
  4. Add, edit, and remove components.
  5. Edit text and images.
  6. Add or rename pages and sections.
  7. Save.
  8. Preview.
  9. Publish.

### 2. AI-Driven Onboarding Flow (Step 2)
- **Category Selection:** User chooses a business category and must tick a box acknowledging OpenAI terms and conditions (the setup flow is AI-driven).
- **Alternative:** Users can skip the AI flow and choose a template directly via the "use templates instead" link.
- **Information Steps (up to 4):**
  1. Business name and details (varies by category — e.g., restaurant vs. toy store will be asked different questions).
  2. Keywords/sentences about the business (up to 3 items) — used as the basis for AI-generated website text.
  3. Contact details (can be skipped and added later).
  4. Writing perspective — singular or plural (e.g., "I design" vs. "We design").

### 3. Templates and Colour Themes (Step 3)
- **Template:** Determines the overall design (layout, fonts). All templates are fully customisable.
- **Colour Theme:** Comes with a suggested theme to ensure consistent button, background, and accent colours across all pages.
- **Options:**
  - Recommended colour combinations are listed at the top.
  - Additional options available under "Other colours" dropdown.
  - "See all templates" link provides access to the full predesigned template library.
- **Tip:** Clicking **Back** and changing entered information will generate a different template suggestion.

### 4. Page Layout — Header, Footer, and Sections
- Each page consists of a **header**, a **footer**, and at least one **section**.
- Content in the header and footer appears on **all pages** (e.g., menu/logo in header, contact info in footer).
- Sections are the main content areas between header and footer.

### 5. Working with Components (Step 4)
- **Add:** Drag components from the left menu onto the workspace. Red alignment lines indicate positioning relative to other components; red area indicates overlap.
- **Edit:** Double-click a component to open its settings (style, size, link targets). Orange handles on corners/sides allow resizing by dragging.
- **Remove:** Select the component → press Delete on keyboard, or click the three-dots-circle icon → Delete.

### 6. Editing Text and Images (Step 5)
- **Text:** Double-click to edit. A built-in **Writing Assistant** is available to help write or revise website text.
- **Images:**
  - Template images are stock photos — can be kept or replaced.
  - Click an image → click **Replace image** → select from library or click **Upload** (top right) to upload from computer → click **Add to page**.

### 7. Managing Pages (Step 6)
- **Add a page:** Pages menu (top bar, blue background) → click **+ Add new page** → follow instructions.
- **Rename a page:** Pages menu → click the circle/three-dots icon on the page → **Settings** → edit page name → **Save**.
- The page name appears in the navigation menu and browser tab.

### 8. Managing Sections (Step 6)
- **Add a section:** Hover over the **plus sign** on the orange line where you want the section → click **Add section** → choose from the left-side panel.
- **Rename a section:** Select the section → click **Edit section** tab (top or bottom) → **Section title** → enter new title.
- **Anchor links:** Can link from the menu directly to a specific section — useful for one-page websites.

### 9. Save, Undo, and Backup (Step 7)
- **Save frequently** to avoid losing changes.
- **Undo/Redo:** Use the arrows in the top bar.
- **Limitation:** Deleting a page **cannot be undone**.
- **Backup and Restore (Premium only):** Built-in feature that automatically creates a backup every time the site is saved, allowing rollback to previous versions.

### 10. Preview (Step 8)
- Click **Preview** in the top bar to see the site as visitors will see it.
- Navigate through the menu to check all pages.
- Check the **mobile version** to ensure responsive design.
- Click **Back to editor** to return and make changes.

### 11. Publishing (Step 9)
- Click **Publish** in the top bar → confirm by clicking **Publish** again.
- Publishing may take a few minutes depending on page count and content.
- **Verify:** Click the domain name in the top-right menu to view the live site.
- **Troubleshooting:** If the site doesn't appear after publishing, check that the [start page is set correctly](https://help.one.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000306625).

## Key Differences: Classic Website Builder vs. Aida AI Website Builder
- **Classic:** Template-based, drag-and-drop, manual component placement, AI-assisted onboarding and Writing Assistant for text.
- **Aida AI:** Conversational AI-first — describe what you want in plain language, Aida generates layout/copy/structure, visual editor for fine-tuning.
- They are **separate products** with separate plans and pricing.
